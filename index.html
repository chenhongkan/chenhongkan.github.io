我是陈洪侃，北京大学信息管理系博士二年级学生，师从周庆山教授与步一长聘副教授。 我的本科与硕士阶段同样就读于北京大学信息管理系，期间受教于步一长聘副教授与黄文彬长聘副教授。

我的研究聚焦于一个长期问题：如何更科学地识别、评价与预见高创新性的科研活动？ 该问题贯穿科学活动的两个基本层次——成果与人。成果层面，关注何种研究工作具备突破性或颠覆性潜力， 以及这种潜力是否能够在早期加以识别；人与团队层面，关注怎样的合作结构、知识背景与组织模式更易催生创新性成果。 围绕这一主线，我主要从三个方向开展工作：（一）创新性成果的识别方法与评价框架；（二）知识流动、学科交叉与科学结构变迁； （三）科研合作的模式、结构与创新绩效。

与此同时，我也常将日常中遇到的“新奇之事”带回研究工作里，比如在思索游戏排位为何同时具备显性分与隐藏分时， 我意识到ELO模型的核心逻辑可以迁移至热点主题的技术识别中。同样，信息学竞赛的经历也让我总想着把算法竞赛中的经验应用于学术研究。 由此出发，我着手优化了引文网络零模型的构造算法，使大规模网络分析在统计推断层面有了更高效的实现方案，相关改进工作仍在继续推进中。

I am Hongkan Chen, a second-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Information Management at Peking University, supervised by Dr. Qingshan Zhou and Dr. Yi Bu. I also completed my bachelor’s and master’s degrees at the same department, advised by Dr. Yi Bu and Dr. Win-bin Huang.

My research revolves around a long-standing question: How can we identify, evaluate, and anticipate highly innovative scientific activities more scientifically? This question operates at two fundamental levels of scientific practice—outputs and actors. At the output level, I investigate which research works demonstrate breakthrough or disruptive potential and whether such potential can be detected early. At the actor level, I examine which collaborative structures, knowledge backgrounds, and organizational modes are more likely to foster innovative outcomes. Along this line, I focus on three interconnected directions: (1) methods and frameworks for identifying innovative research outputs; (2) knowledge flow, interdisciplinary research, and the dynamics of scientific structure; and (3) research collaboration patterns, team structure, and innovation performance.

I also enjoy bringing curiosity-driven observations into my research. For example, while thinking about why competitive gaming rankings involve both visible scores and hidden MMR, I realized that the logic of the ELO model could be transferred to the identification of hot research topics. Likewise, my background in the Olympiad in Informatics has led me to explore how algorithmic insights might inform academic research — which prompted me to improve the construction algorithms for citation network null models, enabling more efficient randomization of large-scale networks for statistical inference. Further refinements are still in progress.
